# Interface design

This demo applies the shared **engineering routing desk** family to synthetic quality inspection. The paper-and-ink treatment references a controlled inspection sheet, while the signal palette is limited to status and tolerance outcomes.

The signature view combines a dimensional profile drawing, measurement ledger, and tolerance summary. Geometry is authored in the interface and does not reproduce a real component, customer drawing, or factory process. The layout preserves the same evidence hierarchy on mobile.

All records, dimensions, identifiers, tolerances, and drawings are synthetic.

A credited, licensed stock photograph of generic measuring tools adds visual context. It does not depict a real component, facility, result, or production workflow.
