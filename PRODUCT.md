# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Inferred: engineering and quality teams reviewing synthetic dimensional scan records.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate typed scan intake, tolerance evaluation, repository/service separation, and operational
quality reporting without exposing any real employer process or data.

## Positioning

The demo shows the modernization mechanism and queryable record flow while replacing the entire
industrial domain with public-safe synthetic terminology.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Synthetic part IDs, projects, operators, measurements, and geometry only.
- No employer, customer, endpoint, database schema, image, or proprietary workflow.
- Active-development status remains visible.

## Product Principles

- Store measurements, not secrets.
- Evaluate tolerance deterministically.
- Keep list queries lightweight.
- Make quality state visible at a glance.
